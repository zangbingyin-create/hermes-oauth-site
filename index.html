Hermes Forms

Hermes Forms is a personal automation app used by its owner to read and organise their own Gmail messages, Google Forms responses and Google Sheets data. It is not offered as a public service and has no other users.

Privacy policy

This app accesses only the Google account of its owner. Data retrieved through Google APIs (Gmail, Forms, Sheets, Drive) is processed locally on the owner's own computer and is never sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. No analytics or advertising services are used.

Terms of service
